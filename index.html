How it works

Group, game, go.

One organiser sets it up, everyone else taps Join. From an empty group to settled-up in five steps.

  1. 01
    Create a group

    Start a group for your crew and add the regulars. Added once, in for good. Every game you post reaches all of them.

  2. 02
    Create a game

    Say it out loud: sport, time, venue, how many you need. The assistant drafts it. Confirm, and it lands on every member’s home screen.

  3. 03
    Race for slots

    Slots are first come, first served. Members tap Join wherever they are, and the line-up fills itself.

  4. 04
    Show up & play

    Everyone knows who’s coming, when and where. No roll call in the chat the night before.

  5. 05
    Payment confirmed

    Tick off who has settled the court fee, so nobody spends the week chasing people for money.

FAQ

What is ZeroZero?+

A free Android app for running and joining local sports games. Create a group for your crew, post a game by voice, and your regulars tap Join. It is not a court-booking app and there is no way to join games with strangers.

Can I find a court or play with strangers?+

No. ZeroZero does not list venues, book courts, or match you with people you don't know. Every game happens inside a group of players who already know each other.

Do I need an invite?+

Not to start. Create your own group and add your regulars, and that's the usual way in. Joining someone else's group does need an invite from its admin, and once you're in, every game that group posts is yours to join.

Do I need a password?+

No. Enter your email and we send a 6-digit code, or sign in with Google or Apple. You stay signed in.

Do I have to fill in a form to post a game?+

No. Tell the assistant “Badminton tomorrow 6 PM at Smash Arena in Drop Shots” and it drafts the game for you to confirm. Sport is whatever your crew plays: badminton, football, padel, cricket, basketball, volleyball, tennis, pickleball and more.

Android only?+

At launch, yes. Android first. We're keeping it focused so the experience is solid before expanding.

What about court fees?+

ZeroZero never moves money. Players settle up the way they already do: cash, UPI, bank transfer. The organiser ticks off who has paid so the list is up to date and nobody gets chased.
